BIG PLATE NETWORK WEBSITE PROTOTYPE
===================================

CONTENTS
- index.html: Homepage and page sections
- styles.css: Branding, layout, responsive design
- script.js: Mobile menu, article search/filter, demo article modal and demo submission form
- assets/bpn-mark.svg: Editable SVG placeholder mark
- assets/article-placeholder.svg: Article image fallback
- privacy.html and terms.html: Draft placeholder pages that must be reviewed before launch

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file to a folder on your Windows laptop.
2. Open the BigPlateNetwork folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in your browser.
4. Internet access is needed for the externally hosted Unsplash photos. If a photo fails, the page still works; a local fallback exists for submitted demo stories.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- This is a front-end prototype, not a production CMS.
- The article submission form stores entries only in localStorage in the current browser.
- Entries are not sent to an editor, server, email inbox or other user.
- localStorage is not secure authentication or a reliable database.
- Do not collect real confidential information through the demo form.
- There is no real user login, admin dashboard, live radio stream, e-commerce, email delivery or server-side moderation.
- Before launch, add a secure backend/CMS, database, authentication, role-based permissions, server-side validation, spam protection, backups, privacy/cookie compliance and production hosting.
- Replace YOUR_EMAIL@example.com with the actual business contact email.
- Sample article text is explicitly illustrative and must be replaced with verified, approved reporting.
- The privacy and terms pages are placeholders, not final legal policies.
- Use only images, logos and fonts you own or have permission to use.
