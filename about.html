01 · Who We Are

Who We Are

Big Plate Network is a global enterprise built on one powerful belief: great ideas can come from anywhere, talent exists in every community, and everyone deserves the opportunity to make their voice heard.

We are building a connected family of brands that brings together media, creativity, culture, fashion, food, technology, events, and international trade under one shared vision—to connect people, unlock potential, and create opportunities that reach beyond borders.

Our ambition goes beyond building businesses. We want to build platforms that help people turn ideas into action, transform creativity into opportunity, share their stories with the world, and connect with audiences and communities they might never otherwise reach.

From a young creator with a fresh idea to an emerging artist, storyteller, entrepreneur, designer, or innovator, we believe that potential should never be limited by geography, background, or lack of visibility.

Through our growing network, we aim to bring people and perspectives together, celebrate the richness of different cultures, encourage innovation, and open new pathways for collaboration and growth.

Big Plate Network is more than a family of brands. It is a shared vision for a world where ideas travel further, talent finds opportunity, and creativity brings people together.

02 · Our Story

Our Story

A Vision Born to Reach Beyond Borders

The world is filled with extraordinary ideas, untapped talent, powerful stories and people with the potential to make a difference. Yet, too often, those ideas remain unseen, those voices go unheard, and those talents never receive the opportunity they deserve.

Big Plate Network is being built to help change that.

Our story is driven by a bold belief: opportunity should not be limited by geography, background or access. A great idea can come from anywhere. Creativity has no borders. And the right platform can help transform an individual's vision into something that reaches and inspires the world.

We set out to build more than a single business. We envisioned a connected global network of brands, platforms and initiatives that could bring people, industries and ideas together under one shared purpose.

Through media, we aim to give stories a wider reach and perspectives a place to be heard. Through festivals and creative initiatives, we want to help emerging talents showcase their abilities. Through fashion, food, technology, events and international trade, we seek to explore new ways to inspire innovation, build connections and create opportunities.

Every division will have its own identity and purpose, but together they will contribute to something greater: a network that connects creativity with opportunity and local ideas with global possibilities.

Our ambition is to build a platform where a young creator can be discovered, an emerging entrepreneur can find new possibilities, a storyteller can reach a wider audience, and people from different cultures can learn from one another.

We recognise that building this vision will take time, commitment, collaboration and continuous growth. We are building for the long term, with the ambition to develop brands and initiatives that create meaningful value for people and communities around the world.

Big Plate Network is more than a business ambition. It is a vision for connection, creativity and possibility—a growing family of brands united by the belief that when people are given the opportunity to share what they can do, the world gains something valuable.

05 · Our Structure

Why We Are an Umbrella Network

One Network. Multiple Brands. One Shared Vision.

Big Plate Network is the umbrella organisation that brings together a growing family of brands, businesses and initiatives under one shared vision.

We believe that creativity, innovation and opportunity are not limited to a single industry. That is why we are building a network that reaches across media, fashion, food, technology, festivals, events and international trade creating different pathways through which people, ideas and businesses can grow.

Each division within Big Plate Network is designed to have its own identity, goals, audience and area of expertise. While every brand will develop in its own direction, all will remain connected by the values and long-term vision of the wider network.

This structure allows us to explore opportunities across industries, encourage collaboration between our divisions, develop new ideas and build partnerships that may create value beyond the boundaries of any single brand.

As Big Plate Network grows, new divisions and initiatives may join the family where they align with our purpose and contribute to our broader ambitions.

We are building more than a collection of businesses. We are building a connected ecosystem where different ideas and industries can complement one another, share opportunities and work towards a common goal.

One vision brings us together. Different brands allow that vision to grow.

07 · Our Family of Brands

Our Family of Brands

Different Divisions. Shared Values. One Global Vision.

Big Plate Network brings together a growing family of brands, each created to explore opportunities in a different industry while contributing to our shared mission of connecting people, empowering talent and creating possibilities.

Every division has its own identity, purpose and audience. Together, they form a connected network designed to encourage creativity, innovation, collaboration and sustainable growth.

Big Plate Media

Our media division is dedicated to storytelling, photography, videography, broadcasting, news, television and podcasting. Its purpose is to bring stories to wider audiences, highlight different perspectives and create platforms where voices and ideas can be heard.

Big Plate Festival

Our festival and creative initiatives division aims to celebrate arts, crafts, culture and creative expression. It seeks to encourage emerging talent, create opportunities for young creatives and bring people together through shared experiences.

Big Plate Clothing

Our fashion division is focused on developing distinctive clothing and lifestyle products that express creativity, individuality and cultural identity while connecting with audiences across Africa, Europe and beyond.

Big Plate Kitchen

Our food division aims to explore food, culinary creativity and opportunities within the food and beverage industry, celebrating flavours, cultures and experiences that bring people together.

Big Plate Tech

Our technology division will explore innovative ideas, digital solutions and technology-driven opportunities that can help people, businesses and communities connect, create and grow.

Big Plate Events

Our events division aims to develop experiences, gatherings and occasions that connect people, celebrate ideas and create opportunities for collaboration, entertainment and community engagement.

Big Plate Importation

Our international trade division aims to explore importation, sourcing and cross-border business opportunities, helping connect products, markets and business possibilities across countries.

Growing Beyond Today's Possibilities

These divisions represent the foundation of our wider vision. As Big Plate Network develops, we may introduce additional brands, services and initiatives that align with our values and open new opportunities.

Each division will grow according to its own goals and market, while remaining connected to the purpose and principles of the wider network.

One network. Many possibilities. A shared vision for the future.

08 · Our Values

Our Values

The Principles Behind Everything We Build.

At Big Plate Network, our values are the foundation of our identity. They guide how we develop our brands, work with people, approach opportunities and contribute to the communities around us.

1. Creativity

We believe in the power of imagination and original thinking. We encourage fresh ideas, creative expression and the courage to explore new possibilities.

2. Innovation

We embrace progress, new perspectives and better ways of doing things. We aim to evolve with the world around us while creating solutions and opportunities that make a meaningful difference.

3. Opportunity and Empowerment

We believe talent exists everywhere. We aim to create pathways that help people develop their abilities, share their ideas, gain visibility and move closer to their goals.

4. Integrity and Accountability

We believe trust is earned through honesty, responsibility and consistency. We are committed to acting with integrity, respecting our commitments and taking responsibility for our decisions.

5. Collaboration and Community

We recognise that meaningful progress often happens when people work together. We value partnerships, shared knowledge, mutual respect and the connections that help individuals and communities grow.

6. Cultural Respect and Inclusion

We celebrate the diversity of people, cultures and perspectives. We aim to create opportunities where different voices are respected, ideas can be exchanged and people can contribute without unnecessary barriers.

7. Excellence and Continuous Growth

We strive to improve, learn from experience and deliver work we can be proud of. We understand that building a lasting global network requires patience, discipline, adaptability and a commitment to quality.

8. Positive Impact

We want our growth to mean more than business success. We aspire to create value for people, support emerging talent and contribute positively to the communities and industries connected to our work.

These values will guide Big Plate Network as we build new brands, develop partnerships and expand into new markets.

Our values define how we grow, how we work and the legacy we aim to create.

09 · Our Commitment to the Next Generation

Our Commitment to the Next Generation

Inspiring Young Minds. Unlocking Potential. Creating Tomorrow’s Opportunities.

As Big Plate Network grows, we hope to develop initiatives and partnerships that contribute to education, creativity, entrepreneurship and community development across different countries.

Our goal is not to promise success to every person, but to help create more opportunities for people to learn, participate, develop and move forward.

We believe every young person deserves the chance to discover what they can become and the opportunity to show the world what they can do.
